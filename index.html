One core, two flavors

Two flavors. One core.

Newsk Lite is the headless core: the GoNewsk scraper, MiniLM embeddings and pglite on Wolfi. 114 MB, Pi-ready, stateless — results stream to Cloudflare.

Newsk Full adds the Openbox desktop, the EmDash editor and the Harper grammar checker for writing and publishing from the same machine.

Both flavors share one core and one package repo. Build once with melange and apko, run anywhere from a Pi to a server.

Asked about Newsk

Before you ask.

Is Newsk really free?

Yes. MIT and GPL-licensed code, open datasets, open methods. Bring your own hardware.

What hardware do I need?

A Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 with a 32 GB card runs the Lite flavor. Anything with a browser reads the site.

Where does the data live?

Sources in NewskList/NewskData on GitHub; scraped JSONL in R2; nothing of yours leaves your machine unless you upload it.

Can I contribute?

Yes — issues and pull requests at github.com/labsbytir. Docs fixes count as contributions.

Who runs Newsk?

tir labs inc., created by github.com/pennytir. It is a News initiative, not a newsroom.

Which license?

Scraper MIT, editor GPL-3.0/2.0, datasets CC with attribution. See each repo.
